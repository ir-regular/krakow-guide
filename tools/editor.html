Coordinates
Draw a shape to see its coordinates here.
How to use it Click the map to place corner points. Once you have three or more, click the first point again (it turns solid) or press Finish shape to close it. Drag any point to nudge it — even after finishing. Double-click a point to remove it. Paste the result as area: on a district or place (or path: for a line, once that tool exists).
